# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience.

## Our standards

Participants should be respectful, constructive, and open to differing viewpoints. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, and knowingly disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer through the contact listed in the repository owner profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential where possible.
